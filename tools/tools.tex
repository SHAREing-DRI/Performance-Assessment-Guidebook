\chapter{Tools}
\label{chapter:tools}

A lot of our methodology and research questions (cmp.~introductory remarks in
Chapter~\ref{chapter:localisation}) are inspired by VI-HPS and the tools
represented within the consortium.
The visualisation tool Cube \cite{geimer2007} for example shapes the discussion on
localisation.
Consequently, all the tools that can feed into Cube are natural candidates to
answer our most of the questions asked throughout the subsequent in-depth
studies.
However, there are a lot of alternatives out there.
Some of them work for all three flavours, some of them work particularly well
for one or two.


While we discuss some tools in this chapter, this list is not comprehensive and
might over-simplify certain aspects.
We might claim that tools are particular good for one thing while expert users
and their developers know that they can also be used for others.
