\section{MAQAO} 
\label{section:localisation:maqao}

\maqao~has the high-level report but it has this ambition in its DNA to guide the tuning and development.
In that sense, it goes beyond mere analysis and has a slightly different focus than a lot of the other analysis tools.
While \maqao~gets more support for multithreaded and MPI runs with every new generation, its core expertise is the analysis on the core level:
Which loops consume most runtime, vectorise poorly or are not balanced reasonably?
That's the type of prime application area of \maqao.
